## Purpose of the Role
Provide governance, coordination, and continual improvement across ITIL practices to ensure stable, efficient, and value-driven IT services aligned with business objectives.

## Position in Governance
- Reports to: Head of IT Operations / Service Delivery Manager
- Acts as: Process owner / steward across Incident, Request, Change, Problem, CSI
- Escalation path for: Major Incidents, Emergency Changes, Persistent Problems
- Member of: CAB, Service Review Forum, CSI Steering Group

## Core Responsibilities
- Own and mature core ITIL processes
- Ensure SLA/OLA definition, tracking, and adherence
- Enable data-driven decisions via reporting and analytics
- Drive cross-team collaboration and accountability
- Champion automation, shift-left, and knowledge reuse
- Lead governance forums (CAB, Problem Review, Service Review)

## Daily Activities
- Review incident/request/backlog dashboards
- Validate Major Incident status & communications
- Approve / sanity-check today’s Change schedule
- Monitor SLA at-risk items; trigger early intervention
- Support service desk escalations and clarify process usage
- Update stakeholders on critical outages or high-impact trends

## Weekly Activities
- Run Change Advisory Board (CAB)
- Conduct Problem Review / RCA follow-ups
- Lead Operational Service Review (tickets, SLAs, aging)
- Publish KPI snapshot (MTTR, backlog aging, reopen rate)
- Identify automation / knowledge base candidates
- Coach teams on process gaps observed

## Monthly / Quarterly Activities
- Present Executive Service Performance Report
- Refresh risk register & top problem list
- Assess process maturity & CSI backlog status
- Benchmark performance vs targets (trend analysis)

## Key Interfaces
- Service Desk (L1) – execution & feedback loop
- Technical Support (L2/L3) – RCA, resolution quality
- Dev / Engineering – change, deployment coordination
- Security / Compliance – risk & audit alignment
- Business Relationship Managers – demand & satisfaction
- Tooling / Platform Team – workflow & automation enablement


## Automation & Tooling Opportunities
- Auto-categorization / prioritization (AI/ML triage)
- Standard change auto-approval workflows
- Event-to-incident correlation (noise reduction)
- Problem candidate detection from incident clustering
- Knowledge article suggestion during ticket logging
- SLA breach early-warning alerts
- Post-implementation review templates auto-populated


## Success Measures
- SLA compliance % (steady or improving)
- MTTR reduction & incident reopen rate decline
- Reduction in repeat incidents (RCA effectiveness)
- Change success rate & lower emergency change ratio
- Problem closure velocity & preventive action completion
- Self-service adoption & ticket deflection %
- Automation-driven cycle time reduction
- Stakeholder satisfaction (survey / NPS style score)

## Continuous Improvement Focus
- Simplify workflows (remove non–value-add steps)
- Expand proactive monitoring & predictive analytics
- Increase Standard & pre-approved Change coverage
- Embed knowledge reuse into ticket closure criteria
- Prioritize high-impact CSI backlog via ROI scoring
- Align improvement outcomes with business value (cost, risk, agility)
- Regular maturity assessments to steer roadmap
- Foster a culture of data-driven operational decisions
